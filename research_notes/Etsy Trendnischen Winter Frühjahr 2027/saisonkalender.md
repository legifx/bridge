# Etsy Seasonal Demand Calendar, Dec 2026 – May 2027 (Saisonkalender)

Research date: 2026-11-23. Method note: Web search only. Direct page fetches of help.erank.com failed (the host is not in this cloud session's network allowlist), so eRank figures come from search-result excerpts of eRank posts, not from the full pages. Vendor-blog figures (Listify, InsightAgent, Accio, Marmalead) are marked as such and are lower confidence. Dates in the "Inferences" sections that carry no citation were computed from fixed weekday rules (e.g. "2nd Sunday of May"). Check them against an official calendar before publishing.

## 1. Exact dates for 2026/2027 occasions (master calendar)

### Takeaway
The window has an unusual collision: **Valentine's Day 2027 (Sun Feb 14) is also Super Bowl LXI**, the first Super Bowl ever played on Valentine's Day. **Lunar New Year (Sat Feb 6, Year of the Fire Goat) and the start of Ramadan (~Feb 8)** fall in the same week as Galentine's Day. Easter is early (Mar 28), so **UK Mothering Sunday is already on Mar 7**, which puts UK Mother's Day demand right after Valentine's. US and German Mother's Day are both on **May 9**.

### Inferences
- **The Valentine's/Super Bowl collision (Feb 14) is the defining quirk of 2027.** Couple gifts compete with game-day party goods for the same weekend. Combined niches look like a real opportunity: "football + love" shirts, couples' game-day apparel, Valentine's Super Bowl party printables and décor, and "my Valentine is the Super Bowl" humor. Galentine's moves to Saturday Feb 13, which suits party-kit and brunch-printable sellers.
- **Feb 4–14 is a dense 10-day window:** Karneval (DE), Lunar New Year, Ramadan start, Mardi Gras, Galentine's, Valentine's, Super Bowl. All listings for these occasions should be live by early-to-mid January (see Section 4).
- **In Germany, Valentinstag 2027 does not overlap with Karneval** (Rosenmontag is Feb 8). In 2026 it fell on Karnevalssamstag, which HDE linked to a sales loss of more than €100M (see Section 3). 2027 could therefore be a slightly better Valentine's year in DE.
- **Early Easter compresses spring.** UK Mothering Sunday (Mar 7) comes 3 weeks after Valentine's, and Eid, St. Patrick's and Easter all fall within Mar 9–28. UK sellers have almost no gap between Valentine's and Mother's Day, so Mothering Sunday listings should go live in January. eRank observed early Mother's Day searches in the UK as early as January 2026 (Section 4).
- **German Vatertag (May 6) and Muttertag (May 9) are in the same week, right after US Teacher Appreciation Week (May 3–7).** Early May is therefore a triple gifting peak for DE/US sellers.
- **Father's Day (Jun 20, US/UK) is outside the window, but its lead time falls inside it.** Personalized/custom Father's Day listings should be live by about early-to-mid May. That fits the general "~6–8 weeks before" rule cited in Section 4.

### Gaps
- Hanukkah 2026, Passover 2027, Holi 2027 and Administrative Professionals Day were not verified by a source in this session.
- Ramadan and Eid dates are projections and will shift by ±1 day with moon sighting.
- No official NEA page was retrieved for Teacher Appreciation Week (affiliate and school-district sources only).
- Deutsche Post/DHL and Royal Mail Christmas cutoffs for 2026 were not researched.

## 2. Christmas 2026 last-minute demand and the January "new year" reset

### Takeaway
After the US physical cutoffs (Ground Advantage Dec 17, Priority Dec 18, international ~Dec 9), Etsy demand shifts to instant downloads and printable or e-gift formats. January then brings a "reset" spike for planners, budget and goal printables. No Etsy first-party figures were found; the evidence is mostly from third-party tools and vendor blogs.

### Cited Findings
- The physical Christmas cutoffs give the hard boundary for last-minute physical gifts: Dec 17 (Ground Advantage/First-Class), Dec 18 (Priority), Dec 19 (Express), and ~Dec 9 for many international destinations. — [USPS](https://about.usps.com/newsroom/national-releases/2026/0922-usps-recommends-2026-holiday-mailing-shipping-dates.htm)
- Digital-download demand follows "a Q4 gifting build, a January reset spike for planners and budgeting printables, then a summer slowdown" (vendor analysis, low confidence). — [Accio: Etsy digital downloads growth 2026](https://www.accio.com/wow/business-etsy-digital-downloads-growth-2026-trend-statistics.html)
- Etsy digital-planner demand "peaks in August and again across January to April" (vendor analysis). — [Accio: digital planner trends](https://www.accio.com/wow/business-digital-planner-trends.html)
- Top-selling digital planner formats named for 2026: minimalist dated planners for GoodNotes/Notability, ADHD-focused all-in-one planners, and bundles (planner + stickers + budget sheet + dashboard). Digital stickers and planner overlays are called one of the fastest-growing categories (vendor claims). — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/wow/business-digital-planner-trends.html); [ResellReady](https://resellready.co/blogs/news/digital-downloads-that-sell-on-etsy-in-2026)
- Etsy's own market pages show heavy supply of "2026–2027" dated planners, mostly priced at $1–6, often discounted, and many with PLR/MRR bundles. This points to a commoditized, price-competitive niche. — [Etsy market: planners 2026 2027](https://www.etsy.com/market/planners_2026_2027); [Etsy market: trending digital planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/trending_digital_planner_2026_best_seller)
- One third-party guide claims "printable planner" searches rise +140% from December into January. This is unverified and the source does not cite its data. — [eRank Q1 search summary / third-party guides via search](https://www.insightagent.app/blog/best-etsy-keywords-quarterly)
- Style signal: Printify sees 2026 Etsy buyers leaning toward refined, timeless looks ("old-money romance", "châteaucore", "MIDImalism") over novelty, while digital downloads stay steady. — [Printify: Leading trends for Etsy 2026](https://printify.com/blog/leading-trends-for-etsy-sellers-in-2026/)
- Some 2026 Etsy trend pages contain impossible "closed 2026" totals, so that whole class of AI-generated stats pages is unreliable. — (observed in [Accio](https://www.accio.com/wow/business-etsy-digital-downloads-growth-2026-trend-statistics.html) search results)

### Inferences
- **Dec 18–24 last-minute set:** printable gift certificates and "IOU"/experience vouchers, printable or editable Christmas cards, digital family-portrait or illustration commissions delivered as files, personalized digital art (star maps, name prints as downloads), digital party games, kids' "letter from Santa" printables, and digital "gift reveal" cards for experiences or trips. For international buyers, the digital switch starts already around Dec 9.
- **Proposal-season spillover (Dec 24–25 peak):** demand for "she said yes" engagement announcements, engagement party printables and save-the-date templates should follow Christmas and run into January. Bridesmaid/"will you be my bridesmaid" proposal boxes should follow in Jan–Mar as newly engaged couples plan. This is inferred from The Knot proposal-timing data; no Etsy search figure was found.
- **January set:** 2027 dated planners (paper and digital), budget/finance trackers, debt payoff and savings challenges, habit/goal trackers, meal planners, fitness and "Dry January" trackers, home organization labels, and tax-prep worksheets (US tax season starts late January). Undated or "2027–2028" academic planners reduce the risk of dead stock.

### Gaps
- No Etsy first-party data was found on the share of digital-download sales after the Christmas cutoff.
- No search-volume data was found for "dry january", "budget planner", "2027 planner" or "tax planner" on Etsy. The eRank monthly keyword reports for January could not be fetched.
- No data was found on Etsy gift cards/e-gift volume.

## 3. Which niches spike hardest per occasion, with quantitative demand context

### Takeaway
Valentine's Day was by far the strongest Q1 2026 gifting search on US Etsy ("valentines day gift" rose to #4 among all Q1 searches, from #140 a year earlier). Etsy management said Valentine's (love, breakups, Galentine's) and Easter (personalized baskets, toys, gifts) lifted Q1 2026 results. NRF forecasts record US spending for Valentine's ($29.1B), Easter ($24.9B) and Mother's Day ($38B, jewelry the #1 category at $7.5B). German spending, by contrast, is flat to slightly down (HDE).

## 4. Lead times: when searches start rising and when listings must be live

### Takeaway
Etsy listings take weeks to index and gain ranking. Vendors cite a 3–6 week "algorithm lag", and the consensus advice is to list about 6–8+ weeks before an occasion. Valentine's searches start rising as early as November and peak in January (US). The UK Mother's Day and Easter cycle starts showing in January searches. Easter listings should be live by late January.

### Cited Findings
- Listify claims Etsy's algorithm has a **3–6 week lag**, so listings must go live well before the peak (vendor claim, unsourced). — [Listify: Etsy holiday calendar 2026](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/etsy-holiday-calendar-2026)
- eRank: Valentine's upward search trend **"begins as early as November"**.
