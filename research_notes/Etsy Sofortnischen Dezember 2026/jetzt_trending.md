## 1. Which Etsy search terms/products are trending in Oct–Nov 2026, and what concrete products sit behind the Etsy Fall/Winter 2026 Trend Edit?

### Takeaway
"Christmas" is now the #1 US Etsy search (October 2026, eRank), with shirts/sweatshirts, crochet patterns and "digital products" close behind. Etsy's own Fall/Winter 2026 Trend Edit puts its strongest commercial signal on **Family Reimagined**: personalized friend gifts up 1,400% and pet gifting searches doubled. The other themes point to specific products: DIY craft kits (Craftmas), rhinestone/bedazzled ornaments (Merrymaxxing), and ruffle tablecloths (Table Dressing).

### Inferences
- The best fits for a fast launch are personalized friend gifts (POD tumblers, sweatshirts, ornaments, "bestie"/friendship keepsakes), pet gifts, Craftmas kits (DIY ornament-painting kits, bedazzle-your-own ornament or stocking kits, beginner sewing/needlepoint patterns as instant PDFs), and rhinestone/maximalist ornaments. Each has a large Etsy-reported growth number and can be produced quickly.
- Ruffle tablecloths (+841%) are a strong signal, but they need sewing or sourcing capacity. Printable or digital table-setting items (place cards, menus) are a low-capital way into the same trend.
- The eRank lists show "crochet pattern", "digital products" and "png" in the top 10. Digital and pattern demand is massive but also saturated (see Q3).
- Etsy is guiding Q4 growth at only ~5–7% even though personalization is a stated growth driver. Personalization is therefore the platform's tailwind, but the overall pie isn't exploding.

## 2. Personalized Christmas 2026 items with the highest demand (first Christmas, pet, family ornaments, stockings, Secret Santa, teacher/coworker/friend gifts)

### Takeaway
Personalized ornaments are the core Christmas search cluster. Generic "family name", "baby's first Christmas" and "photo" ornaments are extremely crowded (~95k–119k listings each). Pet-specific and specific-moment ornaments (first Christmas in our new home, first Christmas married, new pet parent, breed-specific) face far less competition and line up with Etsy's "pet gifting doubled" signal.

### Cited Findings
- A seller guide cites an Oct 15, 2026 Etsy snapshot of listing counts: family name ornaments **118,705**, baby's first Christmas **114,586**, photo ornaments **96,049** (the most crowded). The same guide says pet designs made up **12.0%** of bestsellers and family designs **9.9%**. — reported in the guide surfaced at [Merchize: best Etsy Christmas ornaments](https://merchize.com/best-etsy-christmas-ornaments/) (attribution to this exact page is from a search summary; verify)
- An August 2026 keyword read found **984** listings competing for "poodle christmas ornament", which is low for Etsy. This is the breed-specific long-tail angle. — [ListifyAI: poodle christmas ornament](https://www.listifyai.net/keyword/poodle-christmas-ornament)
- Live pet first-Christmas listing prices range from about $9.78 (personalized bauble) to $59.99 (needle-felted custom dog ornament). — [Merchize](https://merchize.com/best-etsy-christmas-ornaments/) / Etsy listings surfaced in search
- Pet "first Christmas" ornaments are pitched as the ideal gift for new pet parents (AOL/Etsy feature). — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/cute-etsy-paw-fect-gift-161652022.html)
- Top Etsy Christmas searches reported for 2026 include "personalized Christmas ornament", "custom family ornament" and "personalized stocking". — [Printify Etsy search trends 2026](https://printify.com/blog/top-etsy-searches/)
- Etsy's own 2026 gift pages feature year-dated items such as "Personalized New Home Christmas Ornament 2026" and a custom address house keepsake. — [Etsy: 2026 Gift Ideas](https://www.etsy.com/market/2026_gift_ideas)
- Customily (a personalization software vendor) advises building around specific moments (first Christmas married, baby's first Christmas, new home) rather than the broad "personalized ornament". It names stockings, tree toppers, pillows, blankets, doormats, pet photo ornaments, custom pet portraits and personalized pet stockings as strong 2026 items. — [Customily: Personalized Christmas Gift Trends 2026](https://www.customily.com/post/personalized-christmas-gift-trends-what-shoppers-are-actually-searching-for)
- Gelato reports that searches for "custom gifts" and "personalized name items" rose over 30% on creative marketplaces in late 2026. The primary source was not verifiable. — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/most-purchased-item-for-christmas)
- Teacher/coworker: Gelato puts typical personalized mug pricing at $10–15, a fit for teacher and coworker gifts. Vistaprint suggests notebooks, tote bags and drinkware for employees. Both are generic vendor content. — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/most-purchased-item-for-christmas); [Vistaprint](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/personalized-christmas-gifts)
- Personalized friend gifts are up 1,400% (Etsy Trend Edit, see Q1). — [Etsy Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/etsy-success-300/topic/etsy-insights-etsy-trend-edit-fallwinter-26-190171/)

### Inferences
- Best physical bets this week, ordered from least to most competition: (1) breed- or species-specific pet ornaments and pet stockings with a name; (2) "first Christmas in our new home 2026" or "first Christmas married/engaged 2026" ornaments; (3) personalized friend ("bestie", "friendsmas") ornaments and keepsakes. Avoid generic "family name ornament" and "baby's first Christmas" unless you have a strong design edge, because of the ~115k listings each.
- Year-dated "2026" items (ornaments, first-Christmas products) only sell until about Dec 17–19 for US delivery, so they must go live immediately. Their sales window ends abruptly.
- The physical cutoffs (USPS Dec 17–19) mean any made-to-order item needs a production time of ≤3–5 days to capture the peak week of Dec 1–14.

### Gaps
- No 2026 search-volume data was found for "Secret Santa"/"Wichteln", teacher gifts or coworker gifts specifically on Etsy.
- No reliable German/EU-specific Etsy (Wichteln) data turned up in English-language searches.
- The 1,400% friend-gift figure is Etsy's; whether it refers to searches or sales is reported as searches, but one source raised the question without answering it.

## 3. Digital products that sell after shipping cutoffs (printable games, gift certificates, cards, advent, NYE, 2027 calendars/planners)

### Takeaway
"Digital products" is a top-5 search term on Etsy US and UK (eRank Oct 2026), so demand is real. Christmas and NYE printable game bundles are dominated by incumbents with 6k–11k reviews and $4 price points. The opening is the **2027-dated** angle: most current NYE listings are still titled "2026", and "Goodbye 2026 / Hello 2027" year-in-review or resolution printables are thin.

### Cited Findings
- eRank Oct 2026: "digital products" is in the US top 4 and #1 in the UK; "png" and "digital" also rank in the US top 10. — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy/)
- A Christmas game bundle with **6.1k reviews** sells for about $4.84. A New Year's game bundle with **11.3k reviews** is $4.00 after a 50% discount. This shows heavy incumbent competition. — [Etsy: New Year Party Games](https://www.etsy.com/market/new_year_party_games); [Etsy: New Years Games](https://www.etsy.com/market/new_years_games)
- Many NYE game listings still carry "2026" in the title (e.g. "Printable New Year's Eve Game 2026"). — [Etsy listing 919369474](https://www.etsy.com/listing/919369474/printable-new-years-eve-game-2026-o)
- Only a few listings reference 2027 directly, e.g. a "Goodbye 2026, Hello 2027" year-in-review game and a "2026 Year in Review and 2027 Resolutions" printable. — [Etsy: New Years Eve Year in Review Printable](https://www.etsy.com/market/new_years_eve_year_in_review_printable)
- New Year party planner listings have far fewer reviews, with the top one around 95. — [Etsy: New Eve Plan](https://www.etsy.com/market/new_eve_plan)

### Inferences
- Last-minute digital is a strong window from about Dec 15 to Dec 31. Once USPS cutoffs pass (Dec 17–19), buyers can only get instant downloads, printable gift certificates/"coupons" or e-cards. List these now so they gain ranking history before the spike.
- A generic "Christmas printable games bundle" is saturated (incumbents have 6k–11k reviews). Better ways in: (a) 2027-dated NYE products (year-in-review 2026, 2027 resolutions/vision board, "2027 predictions" game); (b) niche-audience games (office party, teens, family Zoom); (c) editable "gift voucher/experience certificate" templates (Canva/PDF) tied to friend or pet gifts.
- Craftmas (DIY +8,500%) supports instant-download patterns: beginner sewing, needlepoint, crochet/amigurumi. But "crochet pattern" is a top-3 search with very high competition, so niche designs are needed.

### Gaps
- No 2026 search-growth numbers were found for printable gift certificates, digital advent calendars or printable Christmas cards on Etsy.
- No sales-volume data, only review counts as a proxy.
- Etsy's own seller holiday cutoff guidance for 2026 was not found (etsy.com is blocked).

## 4. January 2027 surge products to list now (planners, Valentine's, Dry January, habit trackers)

### Takeaway
Based on January 2026 data, "valentines day gift" jumps to #1 on Etsy US and UK in January (it was absent from December's top 1,000). Planners and digital planners are the strongest secondary January category. Both should be listed in December to build listing history before the surge.

### Cited Findings (2026 data from last January, flagged as the prior cycle)
- eRank January 2026 US: "valentines day gift" was #1, flagged as a "screamer" that did not rank in December's top 1,000. Other top-20 terms included stickers, gift, shirt and jewelry. — [eRank: Top Keywords on Etsy in January 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-keywords-on-etsy-january-2026/)
- eRank January 2026 UK: "valentines day gift" was #1; "digital products" and "self care gift" were also in the top 20. — [eRank: UK Top Etsy Keywords January 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/the-uks-top-etsy-keywords-january-2026/)
- eRank January 2026 Australia: "planner" climbed to #15 and "digital planner" rose from #22 to #16. — [eRank: Australia January 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/australias-top-etsy-keywords-january-2026/)
- eRank Q1 2026: four other Valentine-related searches were in the Q1 top 20 a year earlier, but in 2026 only the exact "valentines day gift" made it. Valentine searches are concentrating on one head term. — [eRank: Top Etsy Searches Q1 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/top-etsy-searches-q1-2026/)
- Printify says New Year planners lead Q1, followed by Valentine's gifts. — [Printify](https://printify.com/blog/top-etsy-searches/)

### Inferences
- Concrete products to list by mid-December: 2027 digital planners (GoodNotes/iPad), 2027 budget trackers, printable 2027 calendars, habit/fitness trackers, "word of the year 2027" prints, and personalized Valentine's gifts (couples items, Galentine's/friend items that match the friend-gift trend, pet Valentine's).
- "Self care gift" in the UK January top 20 supports Dry January/wellness bundles, but no direct Dry January Etsy data was found.

### Gaps
- No 2026/2027 data for "Dry January" or "2027 planner" search volumes on Etsy.
- No current competition counts for 2027 planners. Digital planners are widely reported as saturated, but no 2026 figure was found to confirm it.

## 5. Black Friday/Cyber Monday 2026 data (Etsy, Adobe, Salesforce)

### Takeaway
Adobe forecasts record but moderate growth: $12.9B Black Friday (+9.2%), $15.1B Cyber Monday (+6.2%), and $275.1B for the US online season (Nov 1 to Dec 31, +6.7%), with mobile at 57.4%. These are forecasts released Sept 28, 2026; actual results come after Thanksgiving weekend. No Etsy-specific BFCM 2026 category data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Adobe 2026 forecast: Black Friday $12.9B (+9.2% vs. $11.8B in 2025); Cyber Monday $15.1B (+6.2%); Cyber Week $47.5B (+7.4%, 17.3% of the season); full season $275.1B (+6.7%). Electronics is the largest category at $63.3B (+5.9%). Mobile reaches 57.4% of spend (from 56.4%). BNPL is forecast at $807M on Black Friday and $21.3B for the season. Discounts peak at up to 30% off during Cyber Week. — [Nowoka: Adobe $275B forecast](https://nowoka.com/blog/holiday-2026-ecommerce-marketing-plan-adobe-forecast/); [MLQ.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/black-friday-2026-is-nov-27-what-forecasters-expect-shoppers-to-spend-and-where-the-discounts-are/); [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/black-friday-stats) (secondary write-ups of Adobe; Adobe's own release was not retrieved)
- By day: Black Friday is reportedly best for toys, TVs, appliances and apparel; Cyber Monday for electronics and computers. — [MLQ.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/black-friday-2026-is-nov-27-what-forecasters-expect-shoppers-to-spend-and-where-the-discounts-are/)
- Etsy Q4 2026 GMS guidance is $3.46B–$3.52B (~5–7% YoY). Management cites holiday demand variability. — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-q3-2026-earnings/)

### Inferences
- Mobile accounts for over 57% of spend, so listings need thumbnail-legible first photos and personalization fields that are easy to fill on a phone.
- Discounts peak at about 30%, so a new shop launching this week can join Etsy BFCM sales (Nov 27–30) for visibility. Margins on low-ticket POD will be tight.

### Gaps
- No Salesforce 2026 holiday forecast or category data was retrieved.
- No Etsy press release with BFCM 2026 category data was found. Etsy's newsroom was not reachable.
- No actual BFCM 2026 results exist yet (today is Nov 23).
