## 1. Which gift/product trends are viral on TikTok and Instagram in Oct–Nov 2026?

### Takeaway
The best-supported live trend is **"bedazzle / whimsy / maxxing" maximalism** (rhinestones, gems, whimsical decor and bedazzling kits). It took off after a Kylie Jenner post in August 2026. Fortune covered it on Aug 28, and Etsy reports bedazzled kits up 218%. "Maxxing" is also **Dictionary.com's 2026 Word of the Year**. Craft-night/DIY kits, personalized friend and pet gifts, phone and bag charms, gourmand fragrance, and plaid/matching pajamas also have 2026 evidence. Labubu-style blind boxes are **declining sharply** and are trademark-heavy, so avoid them.

### Inferences
- **Top immediate Etsy plays (low IP risk if original):**
  1. *Bedazzle/"merrymaxxing" items*: DIY rhinestone kits (cup, phone case, mirror or ornament bedazzle kits), pre-bedazzled tumblers/cup charms, gem ornaments, sparkly hair clips. This fits the Fortune "rhinestoned cups" signal and Etsy's bedazzled kits +218%.
  2. *Craft-night / "Craftmas" kits*: girls'-night ornament-painting kits, beginner sewing/embroidery/crochet kits, printable craft-party invitations and instructions (digital = instant delivery, good for late December).
  3. *Personalized friend and pet gifts*: "chosen family" ornaments, bestie-name charms, pet-portrait ornaments, pet stockings (friend gifts +1,400%, pet searches doubled).
  4. *Phone and bag charms*: beaded name charms, textured/fur or tassel charms, mini crochet plush charms. Lean toward the newer "textured / single statement charm" direction rather than charm piles.
  5. *"Maxxing" slang merch* (Word of the Year news hook): generic phrases such as "cozymaxxing", "nonnamaxxing" and "merrymaxxing" on mugs, ornaments and sweatshirts. Original wording; trademark status unchecked (see Gaps).
  6. *Operatic Drama*: capelets, brooches (incl. men's), vintage-style jewelry for holiday parties.
- Avoid blind boxes and Labubu-adjacent designs: the demand is falling, and "Labubu" is a Pop Mart trademark and character design. Generic "mystery/blind bag" packaging of a seller's own charms is lower risk, but the evidence that it sells is weak.
- Bag charms are late-cycle. They still work as stocking stuffers but are not a breakout bet.

### Inferences
- **Safe generic angles (original designs, no names, logos, characters or quotes):**
  - *Dune-adjacent*: "desert/dune aesthetic" in sand, ochre and rust: desert-sunset prints, sandworm-free dune landscapes, "desert night" candles, linen/neutral desert decor. **Do not use** "Dune", "Arrakis", "Muad'Dib", "Shai-Hulud", "spice must flow" or Fremen designs (Legendary/WBD and the Herbert estate).
  - *Avengers*: only generic "superhero movie night" or comic-pop art with original characters. **Do not use** Marvel/Avengers names, characters, logos or "Doomsday"-styled lettering (Disney/Marvel are very aggressive on Etsy).
  - *Harry Potter series*: generic "magic school / wizard academy / dark academia": original crest designs, candle-and-owl decor, "potion" labels without HP names, spellbook journals, wizard-hat ornaments, Christmas-Day watch-party kits ("cozy wizard marathon"). **Do not use** Hogwarts, house names (Gryffindor etc.), "Platform 9¾", "Muggle", character names or the HP font (Warner Bros. enforces heavily).
  - *GTA VI*: generic "retro Miami / neon '80s crime-city / sunset palms" aesthetics and "gamer launch survival kit" gifts. **Do not use** GTA, Rockstar, Vice City or Leonida names, the logo style or game art.
  - *Movie-night kits*: popcorn-box sets, personalized ticket-stub ornaments, "Dec 18 double feature" printable bingo (no film names) and couple/family movie-night gift baskets carry the lowest IP risk and fit "Dunesday" plus Christmas-Day streaming.
